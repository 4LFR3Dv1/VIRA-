# Evaluation Guide

The maintained three-minute walkthrough is [JUDGE_WALKTHROUGH.md](JUDGE_WALKTHROUGH.md). The live browser guide is available at [vira.snelabs.space/help](https://vira.snelabs.space/help?lang=en).

## Permanent path

1. Open the guided playback from the browser guide.
2. Confirm the disclosure that the source is a sanitized captured TxLINE fixture.
3. Follow countdown, kickoff, private answer, server lock, eligible signal, shared resolution and ranking.
4. Inspect the evidence view or [`/public/playback`](https://vira.snelabs.space/public/playback).
5. Compare the product path with the repository's replay and judge-playback tests.

## What this path proves

- The production consumer projections can render the complete deterministic journey after the original event.
- Replay preserves ordering, integrity checks and ranking equivalence.
- Captured evidence is not presented as current live delivery.

Hackathon-specific scoring and submission notes belong in the detailed walkthrough; product architecture and commercial material remain in their canonical documents.

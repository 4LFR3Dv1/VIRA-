# Commercial Path

VIRA is currently a live public product and technical flagship. Its commercial direction is a B2B2C participation layer for broadcasters, sponsors, clubs, creators and sports communities. These are proposed commercial formats, not announced contracts or revenue claims.

| Format | Intended buyer | Product value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsored Match Room | Brand or broadcaster | A focused interaction around one fixture |
| Tournament package | Organizer or media partner | A consistent participation layer across a competition |
| Creator Mini League | Creator or community | A recurring match-day social loop |
| White-label experience | Club, app or rights holder | Partner-branded challenge and distribution surface |

The measurable product funnel is `share created → invite opened → guest joined → live answer → result viewed → result shared`. No money, payout, prize or token purchase is part of the current consumer experience.
